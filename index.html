KinematikLab

Most of this started with one car. Working on my Nissan B13 led me into engine management, air-conditioning control, and the question of what I could learn from the ECU while the engine was running.

Nissan B13 · Speeduino

I built the modular harness and crank trigger wheel for the standalone ECU, then used the car to work through wiring and sync faults.

KinematikLab

I started working on the B13 because I wanted to understand its faults well enough to trace them myself. During one no-start, I found power and ground but no spark or injector pulse. Reflowing the crank angle sensor pins got the engine running again for a few months.

Later I built a dedicated harness and moved to a Speeduino ECU. The AC controller and the engine display came out of working on that same car.

Nissan B13 · Speeduino

I built the modular harness and crank trigger wheel for the standalone ECU, then used the car to work through wiring and sync faults.
